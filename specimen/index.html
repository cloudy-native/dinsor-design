Design brief

Warm paper, one green.

A page should feel like a typeset document on warm paper, then like a modern site. Paper, whitespace, and one deep green do the branding.

Size and space create hierarchy. Color does not.

Color

Warm neutrals, one green

Ink green is the only chromatic brand color. Neutrals stay yellow-brown. The red is for errors, not for branding.

Type

Serif for display. Sans for reading.

Source Serif 4 carries headlines, big numbers, and pull quotes, always at weight 500. Source Sans 3 carries everything you read and click. Display drops to 36px below 768.

Locked changes

  • Accent is ink green, not ink blue.
  • Body is a sans at 17px, not a print-size serif.
  • Overlines are tracked sans, not small caps.
  • Tags take a green tint. The paper stays warm.
